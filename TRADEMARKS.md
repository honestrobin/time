# Trademark policy

The code is free software under the AGPL-3.0. The name is not part of that licence. "Honest Robin", "Honest Robin: Time" and the robin mascot identify the project and the service run by its maintainers, so that people know who they are dealing with.

This policy exists for one reason: anyone may run, change and host this software, but nobody else may present their version or their service as ours.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your product **is based on**, **is a fork of**, or **is compatible with** Honest Robin, as long as that is true.
- Use the name to refer to the project in articles, talks, reviews and comparisons.
- Distribute **unmodified** releases under their own name, for example in a package repository or as a container image.
- Run the software for yourself or your company under its own name.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use "Honest Robin", a confusingly similar name, or the mascot as the name or logo of your own product, company or hosted service.
- Distribute a **modified** version under the name. Rename it, and say what it is based on.
- Suggest that we endorse, sponsor or operate your product or service.
- Register the name or a similar one as a domain, trademark or social media account.

## Hosting it for others

You may sell hosting of this software. Describe it truthfully ("managed hosting of the open-source Honest Robin: Time"), under your own brand, and without the mascot as your logo. If you host a modified version, the AGPL also requires you to offer your users the source of your modifications.

## Questions

Write to **hello@honestrobin.com**. If you're unsure whether a use is fine, ask: we'd rather say yes than see a project renamed later.

This policy may change as the project grows; the current version is the one in this repository.
